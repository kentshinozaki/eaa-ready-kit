How it works

  1. Open the kit in any modern browser. No account, no install, nothing sent to a server.
  2. Work through the 50 plain-language checks on your own store and mark each one pass, fail or not applicable.
  3. Assign owners and dates to the fixes the kit creates for each failure.
  4. Fill in your business details and publish the generated accessibility statement on your site.
  5. Download the dated audit record and repeat the check after major changes, at least once a year.

Questions

Does the EAA apply to my shop?

The EAA has applied since 28 June 2025 to e-commerce services offered to consumers in the EU, including sellers based outside the EU. Microenterprises providing services are exempt. Check the rules in the countries you sell to.

How is this different from an accessibility widget?

Overlay widgets add a toolbar to your site but do not fix the underlying issues or produce a statement or a fix plan. This kit helps you find the issues, plan the fixes and document the result.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your results are stored only in your browser's local storage. The kit makes no network requests, and you can export a backup file whenever you like.

Can I use it for client shops?

Yes. The kit keeps a separate project for each shop. Each licence covers one business, so buy one licence for each client shop you assess.

Which standard does it follow?

WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA, the web requirements referenced by the harmonised European standard EN 301 549.
